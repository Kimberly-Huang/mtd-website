01 · WHO to target

A five-tier ladder, built from rules you can read.

Every físico customer is scored on five independent axes and sorted into bands. No machine learning, no black box: each input is a named column, and each band is validated out-of-time against who actually converted the following month.

Click any band to drill into its profile

Conversion lift vs base rate

Físico → omni, out-of-time validation (score month T, observe T+1). Base rate 0.16%.
The score

Five orthogonal axes, no ML

Max pairwise correlation 0.52, so no axis is a proxy for another. Effective weights shown.

AGE was added at sponsor request (weight 0.12). It is independent of the other four (corr ≤0.08) and improves top-decile lift from 2.66× to 2.75×. Usable for ~60% of customers; missing values default to neutral.

02 · WHAT to say

The message is not "try online". It is "here is why online fits you".

The same customers rate their online journey lower than their physical one, so a generic promo fights an experience problem. Instead we map each Label_Cesta persona to the motivation already visible in their basket, then write to that motivation.

The lever

Click & Collect: +57.3 NPS, lowest detractor rate

"Retire na Loja" is online's best-liked mode and it is exactly the O2O behaviour we want to grow. It sidesteps the delivery complaints that dominate online detractors, and it pulls customers into a store where Swift controls the experience.

The timing

Payday and early-month windows

Activation concentrates on payday windows using the CALENDAR business-day flag, and lands 3 to 4 days ahead of the push to respect Swift's campaign lead time. December is the seasonal ceiling: R$89.6M versus R$58.5M in the June trough.

Independent validation

The bands hold on two years of real transactions

We re-checked the ranking against IDENTIFIED_SALES_DOCUMENT, a table the score never touched. Mean purchases per customer: P1 22.8 > P2 18.0 > P3 4.3. The priority ordering survives on actual buying behaviour, not just on the score's own inputs.

The honesty panel

Tested and ruled out

Reported as first-class results, because each one prunes the plan.

✕

QR code as a readiness signal

Failed twice: coverage below 0.4% and headline lift 0.85×, under 1. Not a usable score input, despite being our most promising early hypothesis.

✕

Weather-triggered campaigns

Temperature against physical sales correlates +0.04 (p = 0.73). No signal. Dropped unless rain data arrives, since precipitation is the more plausible driver.

✕

NPS as a churn predictor

Omni detractor 3-month retention 91.4%, promoter 91.2%. NPS is an experience diagnostic, not an early-warning list, so we use it to fix the channel rather than to target people.

✕

Folding multi-store into the score

Top-decile lift dropped 2.66× → 2.52×. We kept the score intact and used multi-store as a band-level filter (P2-Ready) instead.
